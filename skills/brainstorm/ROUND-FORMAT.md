# Round format

Head each round with its phase and its number within that phase (`What · Round 2`, `How · Round 1`). Group the round's questions under **category** headings, each a short label you pick for what its questions share (`Scope`, `Data model`, `Error handling`), and close each category with a horizontal rule. Head each question with its number and title, counting on across categories, then ask it, list its options, and mark your recommendation. When a question has no natural options, give your recommended answer without letters. Word each question so "ok" accepts your recommendation.

```
# What · Round 1

## 🔷 <category>

### ❓ Q1 - <question title>

<the question>

- **A** - <option>
- **B** - <option>

✅ **B** - <your recommendation and the one-line reason>

### ❓ Q2 - <question title>

<the question>

- **A** - <option>
- **B** - <option>

✅ **A** - <your recommendation and the one-line reason>

## 🔷 <another category>

### ❓ Q3 - <question title>

<the question>

- **A** - <option>
- **B** - <option>
- **C** - <option>

✅ **A** - <your recommendation and the one-line reason>

...

---

Reply with letters (`1B 2A`), `ok` to take every recommendation, or your own answer.
```
